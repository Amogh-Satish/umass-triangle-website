About

The only social fraternity in the country built for men in STEM and architecture.

Since 1907

Our History

Triangle was founded on April 15, 1907 at the University of Illinois by sixteen civil engineering students who wanted to build friendships that would last beyond college. They founded it on the values of honor, friendship, character, brotherhood, and courage.

Created first for engineers, Triangle later widened to include men studying engineering, architecture, mathematics, and the physical sciences. That focus is what still sets it apart: it is a social fraternity centered specifically around men in STEM and architecture. Today, more than 31,000 men have been initiated into Triangle nationwide.

Veritas Omnia Vincit

Our Purpose

The purpose of Triangle shall be to maintain a fraternity of engineers, architects and scientists. It shall carry out its purpose by establishing chapters that develop balanced men who cultivate high moral character, foster lifelong friendships, and live their lives with integrity.
