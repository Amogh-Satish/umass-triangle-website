Presidential Address

Hey there,

I’m Harsith Thokala, a Computer Science student, and I’m proud to serve as the first president of Triangle at UMass Amherst. Our chapter was founded in 2025, so we’re new, growing fast, and still writing our story. I joined because I wanted a brotherhood built for people who spend their time in labs, studios, and science buildings. Triangle has done that nationally since 1907, and it’s the only men’s fraternity in the country made specifically for STEM and architecture students.

School comes first here. Our brothers study together, share advice on tough courses, and help each other through hard semesters.

We give back in ways that use what we’re learning, including Triangle’s national partnership with FIRST Robotics. As a young chapter, we’re still choosing our signature events, so new members get a real say in what our service looks like.

Joining also connects you to a network of more than 30,000 brothers in engineering, architecture, and science who know your field and want to see you succeed.

Most of all, Triangle has given me real friendships. Being part of a new chapter means you’re not just joining traditions; you’re creating them. The brothers here now are the founders future members will look back on.

If you’re a STEM or architecture student looking for more out of UMass, come meet us. We’re just getting started, and there’s room for you to help shape what comes next.

Fraternally,

Harsith ThokalaPresident, Triangle Fraternity at UMass Amherst
